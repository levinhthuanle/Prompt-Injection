# Examination Rules — Example University

## General Rules

1. Students must present a valid university ID to enter the examination room.
2. Arrive at least 10 minutes before the scheduled start time.
3. Students arriving more than 30 minutes late may not be admitted.
4. Once you enter, you may not leave and re-enter without supervisor permission.

## Permitted Items

- Blue or black pens (pencil permitted for multiple choice)
- Non-programmable calculators (unless prohibited by instructor)
- University-approved formula sheet (if provided)
- Water in a clear, unlabeled bottle

## Prohibited Items

- Mobile phones, smartwatches, or any electronic communication devices
- Programmable calculators (unless specifically permitted)
- Notes, textbooks, or unauthorized reference materials
- Bags or backpacks near desks (store at the room entrance)

## Academic Integrity During Exams

- No communication with other students during the exam
- Do not look at other students' papers
- Raising hand is the only permitted form of requesting assistance from supervisors

## Special Accommodations

Students with documented disabilities may request extended time or other accommodations. Submit requests to the Accessibility Office at least 2 weeks before the exam.

## Missed Exams

A missed midterm without advance notice results in a zero grade. Medical or emergency documentation submitted within 5 business days may qualify a student for a make-up exam at the instructor's discretion.

Final exams cannot be rescheduled except under extraordinary documented circumstances approved by the Dean's Office.

## Results and Review

Exam results are typically available within 2 weeks. Students may request to review their graded exam paper within 10 days of results being posted.

## Disciplinary Consequences

Violations of exam rules may result in:
- Score of zero for the exam
- Course failure
- Academic probation or suspension
- Permanent academic record notation
