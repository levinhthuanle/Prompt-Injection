# Student Services — Example University

## Academic Advising

Every student is assigned an academic advisor. Advisors assist with:
- Course selection and planning
- Degree progress review
- Career guidance
- Transfer and graduate school advising

Schedule appointments through the Student Portal or visit the Advising Center (Building B, Room 205).

## Financial Aid

The Financial Aid Office assists with:
- Scholarship applications
- Student loans
- Work-study programs
- Emergency funding

Contact: financialaid@example.edu | Building C, Room 101

## Career Services

- Resume workshops (every Tuesday, 3–5 PM)
- Mock interview sessions (by appointment)
- Job and internship board (careers.example.edu)
- Employer networking events each semester

## Health and Wellness

The University Health Center provides:
- General medical consultations (no appointment needed, Mon–Fri 9 AM–5 PM)
- Mental health counseling (appointment required)
- Vaccination services
- Health insurance enrollment assistance

Contact: health@example.edu | Health Center Building

## Housing

On-campus housing is available for first- and second-year students. Applications open 6 months before semester start. Contact housing@example.edu.

## Technology Support

The IT Help Desk is available:
- In person: Technology Center, Ground Floor
- Online chat: support.example.edu (24/7)
- Phone: (555) 300-1000

Services include: password reset, VPN access, software installation, and device troubleshooting.

## Student Clubs and Organizations

Over 80 registered student organizations. Join through the Student Activities Portal. Organizations include:
- ACM Student Chapter (Computer Science)
- Cybersecurity Club
- Data Science Society
- Math Club
- International Student Association

## Accessibility Services

Students with disabilities may register with the Accessibility Office to receive accommodations. Documentation from a qualified professional is required. Services include extended exam time, note-taking assistance, and accessible facilities.

Contact: accessibility@example.edu | Building A, Room 50
