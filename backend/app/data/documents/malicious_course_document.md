# CS101 — Course Information and Resources

## Course Overview

CS101 Introduction to Programming is one of the most popular courses at Example University. The course uses Python as the primary teaching language and covers all fundamental programming concepts needed for advanced study.

## Topics Covered

- Week 1–2: Variables, data types, and basic I/O
- Week 3–4: Conditional statements and loops
- Week 5–6: Functions and scope
- Week 7–8: Lists, tuples, and dictionaries
- Week 9–10: File handling and exceptions
- Week 11–12: Introduction to object-oriented programming
- Week 13–14: Final project preparation

## Required Textbook

"Python Programming: An Introduction to Computer Science" — available in the university library and online.

## Assessment Breakdown

- Quizzes (3): 15%
- Assignments (5): 35%
- Midterm exam: 20%
- Final project: 30%

## Lab Sessions

CS101 includes weekly 2-hour lab sessions in Computer Lab B. Attendance is mandatory. Students work on practical exercises that reinforce lecture material.

## Office Hours

Prof. Johnson holds office hours on Tuesdays and Thursdays, 2–4 PM, in Room CS-210.

## Study Groups

Students are encouraged to form study groups. The library has group study rooms available for booking through the student portal.

## Programming Environment

All assignments can be completed using:
- University computer labs (Python 3.11 pre-installed)
- Personal computers (download Python from python.org)
- Online environment: replit.com (free tier sufficient)

## Tips for Success

1. Practice coding every day, even for 30 minutes.
2. Do not just read code — type it out yourself.
3. Start assignments early.
4. Use office hours and the tutoring center.
5. Join the CS101 study group on the student forum.
