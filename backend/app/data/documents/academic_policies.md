# Academic Policies — Example University

## Grading Scale

| Grade | Points | Range     |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| A+    | 4.0    | 97–100%   |
| A     | 4.0    | 93–96%    |
| A-    | 3.7    | 90–92%    |
| B+    | 3.3    | 87–89%    |
| B     | 3.0    | 83–86%    |
| B-    | 2.7    | 80–82%    |
| C+    | 2.3    | 77–79%    |
| C     | 2.0    | 73–76%    |
| C-    | 1.7    | 70–72%    |
| D     | 1.0    | 60–69%    |
| F     | 0.0    | below 60% |

## GPA Requirements

- Good standing: GPA ≥ 2.0
- Dean's List: GPA ≥ 3.5 (full-time students)
- Academic probation: GPA < 2.0 for two consecutive semesters
- Graduation requirement: minimum cumulative GPA of 2.0

## Attendance Policy

Students are expected to attend all scheduled classes. More than 3 unexcused absences may result in a grade reduction. Absences due to medical reasons require documentation.

## Academic Integrity

Plagiarism, cheating, and unauthorized collaboration are violations of the Academic Integrity Policy and may result in course failure or expulsion. All submitted work must be the student's own unless group work is explicitly permitted.

## Grade Appeals

Students may appeal a grade within 14 days of receiving it. Appeals must be submitted in writing to the instructor, then to the Department Chair if unresolved. The Academic Appeals Committee handles final reviews.

## Incomplete Grades

An Incomplete (I) may be granted when a student cannot complete course requirements due to documented extenuating circumstances. Incompletes must be resolved within 30 days of the following semester.

## Withdrawal Policy

Students may withdraw from a course without academic penalty before the 8th week of the semester. Withdrawals after Week 8 result in a "W" on the transcript. Medical withdrawals may be granted with documentation.

## Transfer Credits

Transfer credits are evaluated by the Registrar's Office. A maximum of 60 transfer credits may be applied toward a degree.
