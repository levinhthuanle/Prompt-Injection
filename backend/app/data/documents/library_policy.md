# Library Policy — Example University Library

## Hours of Operation

- Monday–Thursday: 7:00 AM – 11:00 PM
- Friday: 7:00 AM – 9:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM
- Sunday: 10:00 AM – 11:00 PM

Extended hours during exam periods (check the library website).

## Borrowing Privileges

| User Type      | Books | Loan Period | Renewals |
|----------------|-------|-------------|----------|
| Undergraduate  | 10    | 21 days     | 2        |
| Graduate       | 20    | 42 days     | 4        |
| Faculty        | 30    | 90 days     | Unlimited|

## Digital Resources

All registered students have access to:
- Online journal databases (JSTOR, IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library)
- E-books and e-journals
- Video lecture libraries
- Research tools and citation managers

Access requires your university email login.

## Printing and Computing

- Computer workstations available on all floors
- Printing: 50 free pages per week; additional pages at $0.05/page
- Scanner available on ground floor (free)
- Laptops available for 4-hour loan with student ID

## Study Rooms

Group study rooms (capacity 4–8) available for 2-hour reservations via the library portal. Individual study carrels available on a first-come basis on floors 2–4.

## Quiet Zones

Floors 3 and 4 are designated quiet zones. Talking, phone calls, and loud music are not permitted.

## Fines and Lost Items

- Overdue fines: $0.25/day per item (maximum $10 per item)
- Lost items: replacement cost + $15 processing fee
- Accounts with fines over $20 are blocked from new borrowing
